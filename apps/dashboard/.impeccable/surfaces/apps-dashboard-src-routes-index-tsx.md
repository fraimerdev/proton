## Scope and mode

The public marketing site: `/` plus `/commands`, `/faq`, `/terms`, `/privacy` and the `/invite`
redirect. **Persuade.** The signed-in dashboard is a separate surface in Operate mode and this
brief does not govern it.

## Audience and job

A Discord server owner or head mod deciding whether to trust a bot with ban powers over their
community, usually comparing two or three tabs at once. They arrive holding a specific intent
("I need tickets") rather than shopping for an "all-in-one bot". Success is one click on
**Add to Discord**; the secondary path is a returning admin reaching the dashboard.

## Proof the page may use

Real only: the 27 module manifests, the 111 generated commands, the 88 serverlog events, the 3
OAuth scopes, the 30-day retention constant, and screenshots of the shipping dashboard. PRODUCT.md
forbids inventing server counts, user counts, uptime, testimonials and prices — so the social-proof
slot the category fills with scale metrics stays **empty** rather than filled with inventory
numbers dressed as traction.

## Direction

The category standard, played straight, at MEE6's and Ticket Tool's craft level or better — the
owner's standing preference, reaffirmed 2026-08-25 with those two plus Sapphire named as the
products this sits alongside. The hero follows Sapphire's two-column lockup: wordmark and three
short declaratives left, floating gradient illustrations right.

Because it is the canon, the page earns none of the category's tells. Ruled out and kept out:
eyebrow kickers, gradient-filled headings, the big-number metric strip, same-size icon-card grids,
zero-offset colour halos, and any element carrying both a border and a wide shadow. Emphasis is
weight and size. Capabilities render as a ruled index — the shape the product's own module list
has — not as cards.

## Memorable moment

The three proofs sit together on one darker ground (`--rail`) with the gaps between them tightened,
so the middle of the page reads as one argument in three parts rather than three identical slabs.
It is the only ground change in the scroll.
